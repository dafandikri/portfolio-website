# Third-party asset attributions

## Jurassic Park Gate

- **Title:** [Jurassic Park Gate](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/jurassic-park-gate-f85e89d2c0ef44beb3fe7fd7e72afdc7)
- **Creator:** Mathzilla5335
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Changes:** The original texture maps were resized for web delivery. The merged
  mesh is partitioned at runtime so its two doors can swing from their hinges.
  UVs are duplicated for ambient occlusion, and the model is presented with new
  PBR material settings, eight animated torch flames, local point lights,
  cinematic tone mapping, and a directional shadow setup.

The compact `i` control shown at the edge of the gate scene provides the same
title, creator, source, license, and adaptation notice without typesetting legal
copy into the cinematic frame.
